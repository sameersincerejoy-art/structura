Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Structura Engineering collects through this website and in connection with an engineering enquiry, and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you contact us through the website or by email, you may provide your name, company, work email, phone number, industry, timeline, website, the description of your engineering problem, the evidence you already have, and the decision you need to make.

How it is used

This information is used only to understand your enquiry, respond to it, and determine what level of analysis or engineering work may be justified. It is not used for automated decision-making and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Technical information

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not contain third-party analytics. Standard server logs (IP address, browser, requested page) may be retained for security and reliability purposes by the hosting provider.

Confidentiality

Technical information exchanged in connection with an enquiry is treated as confidential. If your enquiry may involve restricted client information, say so in advance — a mutual non-disclosure agreement can be put in place before technical detail is shared.

Retention and requests

Enquiry information is retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting engagement, or as required by law. To ask what information we hold about you, or to request its correction or deletion, email hello@structura.in.

Last reviewed: November 2026. This page describes current practice and may be updated as the practice grows.
