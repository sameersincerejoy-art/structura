Terms of Engagement

The basis on which Structura Engineering proposes and carries out engineering work.

Enquiries

Website enquiries are used to understand the problem and determine what level of work is justified. An enquiry does not create an engagement. Work begins only when scope, deliverables, schedule and commercial terms have been agreed in writing.

Scope

Every engagement is defined against a specific engineering question and an agreed scope. Work outside that scope — additional load cases, further iterations, additional disciplines — is discussed before it is undertaken.

Engineering judgement and limitations

Analysis results are engineering interpretations of a defined model against defined assumptions and inputs. Key assumptions, limitations and the evidence status of results are documented in the deliverable. Structura provides engineering analysis and technical support; it is not a certification or regulatory authority, and any qualification, approval or compliance activity is defined separately under the applicable programme requirements.

Evidence status

Case studies and public material are labelled by evidence status. Synthetic studies and literature-informed reconstructions use illustrative inputs and are not presented as completed client results. Professional experience is identified separately from work performed by Structura.

Confidentiality

Client information is treated as confidential and is not disclosed to third parties without consent. Confidentiality obligations apply from the first technical exchange, and a mutual non-disclosure agreement can be put in place before restricted information is shared.

Intellectual property

Reports, models and drawings produced for an engagement are delivered for the client's internal engineering use under the terms agreed in the engagement letter. Third-party software licences remain the responsibility of their respective owners.

Liability and professional standards

Liability, warranty and insurance terms are set out in the engagement letter for each project. Work is carried out with the care and competence reasonably expected of a specialist engineering analysis practice.

Last reviewed: November 2026. Project-specific terms take precedence over this general page where the two differ.
